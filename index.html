About ProxiMA Nexus

ProxiMA Nexus is an operational intelligence system that identifies structural weaknesses and converts them into enforceable corrective measures and execution plans.

No insights. No data exposure. Built for environments where accuracy and confidentiality are non-negotiable. It operates without accessing or storing organisational data.
